Microbiology Learning Centre

What is Microbiology?

Microbiology is the scientific study of microorganisms, including bacteria, viruses, fungi, protozoa and microscopic algae.

Classification

Microorganisms can be grouped according to their cellular structure, characteristics and evolutionary relationships.

Gram Staining

Gram staining is a differential staining technique used to distinguish bacteria into Gram-positive and Gram-negative groups.

Microbial Growth

Microbial growth refers to an increase in the number of microbial cells in a population. It is influenced by nutrients, temperature, pH and other environmental conditions.
